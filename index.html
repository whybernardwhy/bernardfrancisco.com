NICE2025 to 2026

Building an AI delivery bench across a partner network

When NICE brought Cognigy conversational AI to its partners, almost none of them had certified engineers to deliver it. I pushed every partner in my portfolio into the certification track and reported progress partner by partner each month, so gaps were visible before they turned into stalled projects.

Result: certified Cognigy Associates went from 1 to 70 and Practitioners from 0 to 6. Partner-led Cognigy engagements grew from 6 in 2025 to 33 in 2026.
NICE2025 to 2026

Picking the right partner for each AI deal

I sit on the buy side of partner bids. Each Cognigy deal was shopped to three or four partners, and I weighed their pricing, level of effort, scope and delivery timeline before a contract was awarded. That gives me a clear view of what wins a partner bid and what makes one fall apart after signing.

NICE2025 to 2026

A support model that kept partner projects moving

Partner engineers kept hitting technical blockers with no clear way into NICE's product and support teams. I set up a support model that routed each blocker to the right expert, which removed the waiting that was holding up partner-led AI implementations.

I also set a go-live evidence standard (signed BRD, customer sign-off and a go-live confirmation) and audited a partner's full case history against it.
