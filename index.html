About Bernard

Nineteen years on the delivery side of customer experience, fifteen of them leading people. He started on the phones in 2007, ran contact center operations for US clients, then moved into bringing new client programs live.

Since 2022 he has worked between the platform and the partners who implement it: deciding which partner wins a deal, then governing scope, risk, change orders and go-live. Most of that work is now conversational AI.

Based in Metro Manila. Open to remote roles and relocation.
